Cybersecurity Bootcamp – Programme Overview

Programme Background

The Cybersecurity Bootcamp was organised in partnership with Brekete Family Academy, a product of Human Rights Radio, as an initiative focused on cybersecurity awareness, knowledge sharing, and digital capacity development.

My Role

I served as a Facilitator for the Cybersecurity Bootcamp, contributing to the delivery of cybersecurity knowledge and guiding participants through relevant learning sessions.

Programme Objectives

- Promote cybersecurity awareness and responsible digital practices.
- Introduce participants to cybersecurity concepts and principles.
- Educate participants on common cyber threats and online vulnerabilities.
- Encourage safe internet usage and digital responsibility.
- Build foundational knowledge of information security.

Programme Documentation

This repository documents the programme, my facilitation role, training resources, instructional materials, practical demonstrations, and relevant programme activities.

Acknowledgement
