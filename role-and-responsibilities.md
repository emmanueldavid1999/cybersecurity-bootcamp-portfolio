# Facilitation Role

## Role

Facilitator — Cybersecurity and Digital Safety Training

## Recognition

The Certificate of Recognition presented to Emmanuel David of Mayo Digital Solutions acknowledges his contribution as a Facilitator at the Cybersecurity and Digital Safety Training held in Abuja from 26–30 October 2026. It recognises knowledge-sharing, technical training and the promotion of ethical cybersecurity practices.

## Facilitation content evidenced by supplied materials

The Cryptography teaching deck contains:

- Learning objectives and explanations of core cryptography concepts
- Comparisons of symmetric and asymmetric encryption
- Explanations of hashing and digital signatures
- Guided OpenSSL encryption, decryption and file-checking activities
- Caesar cipher demonstrations and learner challenges
- Wireshark activities comparing Telnet and SSH
- Group discussion prompts, knowledge checks and an exit assessment
- A suggested five-hour instructor-led teaching plan
