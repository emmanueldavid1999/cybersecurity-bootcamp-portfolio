# Programme Overview

## Programme

Cybersecurity and Digital Safety Training

## Programme details

- **Facilitator:** Emmanuel David
- **Organisation identified on certificate:** Mayo Digital Solutions
- **Partner identified by facilitator:** Brekete Family Academy, a product of Human Rights Radio
- **Location:** Abuja, Nigeria
- **Dates:** 26–30 October 2026
- **Role recognised:** Facilitator

## Programme purpose

The supplied teaching materials focus on building cybersecurity and digital-safety awareness through conceptual instruction and practical demonstrations. The Cryptography module introduces methods used to protect information and secure communications.

## Documented learning areas

The Cryptography materials include encryption and decryption, symmetric and asymmetric keys, hashing, digital signatures, secure communication protocols, OpenSSL exercises, Caesar cipher activities and Wireshark observation.
